PDF Flattener © Nikolai Limbrunner

Turns every page into an image and rebuilds the PDF. Form fields, text layers and DocuSign boxes become part of a flat picture.

Your files never leave your computer. Everything happens locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Only local dependencies: nothing is fetched from a CDN.
  • All outgoing connections are blocked by default.

Flattened copies show a picture of any signature but can no longer be verified as electronically signed. Keep the original for your records.

Text in the output can't be selected or searched. Place signature fields by hand in your signing tool.
